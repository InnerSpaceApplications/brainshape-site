How it works

  1. 1

    Answer 60 quick statements

    Rate how true each one feels. There are no right or wrong answers, and it takes about 10 minutes.

  2. 2

    See your brain shape

    Your style across four areas, a short summary of your top traits, and where you land on 19 scales between two opposite tendencies.

  3. 3

    Get tips that fit

    Concrete, practical ideas for how you work, many inspired by occupational therapy strategies, with the research behind them cited in the app.

Four styles, 19 scales

Each scale sits between two opposite tendencies, like Solo recharger and Social recharger, or Sprinter and Marathoner. There’s no right answer and no “better” end.

Social style

How you recharge, connect, and communicate, and how you read the people around you.

Structure style

How you relate to routine, change, interests, and the rules and details of everyday life.

Sensitivity style

How you take in sound, light, and feelings, and how much room you have before it all fills up.

Focus style

How your attention, energy, memory, and sense of time work, and how you start and finish things.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The test and your core results are free. The Full Profile is an optional one-time purchase, with no subscription.

Is BrainShape a diagnosis?

No. It’s a self-reflection tool for curiosity and wellness. It doesn’t diagnose any condition and isn’t a substitute for a professional assessment or advice.

What happens to my answers?

They stay on your phone. BrainShape has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking, and you can clear your results at any time.

Who is it for?

Anyone 13 or older who’s curious about how their mind works, and why the people around them might experience the same day so differently.
