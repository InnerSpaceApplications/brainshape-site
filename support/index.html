Support

Questions, feedback, or trouble with a purchase? We're happy to help.

Email us: info@brainshape.app
We usually reply within two business days.

How do I restore my Full Profile purchase?

Open BrainShape, finish or open your results, and tap Restore purchase below the unlock button. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it.

Is the purchase a subscription?

No. The Full Profile is a one-time purchase. You can retake the questionnaire as often as you like, and the full profile stays unlocked.

Where are my answers stored?

Only on your phone. BrainShape doesn't create accounts or send your answers or results to us or anyone else. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my results?

At the bottom of your results, tap Clear my results from this phone. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Request a refund through reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or through your Google Play order history for Android.

Is BrainShape a diagnosis?

No. BrainShape is a self-reflection tool for curiosity and wellness. It isn't medical or mental health advice. If you're going through a hard time, call or text 988 (in the US) to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
